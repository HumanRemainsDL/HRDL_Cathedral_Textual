 In digging the grave of Miss Lygon, daughter of Lord Beauchamp, of Powick,who died at Sidmouth in October, 1813, and was buried close to the above monument [Sir Richard Stapeldon’s], the side of Sir Richard's grave fell in, and his entire skeleton was discovered; every part was perfect: from the remains found, it appeared that the corpse had been enfolded in a bull's hide. But little respect was shown to the knight's bones, as the ribs were broken through and turned back, to make room for the new occupant.
