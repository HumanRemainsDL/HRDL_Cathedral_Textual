In advanced age he [John Trelick] became too infirm to perform his official duties, and employed Thomas Trellick, Dean of Exeter, to officiate for him. He died in 1361, and was interred on the north side of the altar of his Cathedral [Hereford Cathedral], where a grave stone marks the spot. An engraved brass effigy with an inscription were removed, and the grave was opened in 1813, when part of a crosier, and a seal of a pope's bull were found, and are preserved in a glass case in the Cathedral
