An effigy of a knight, cross-legged, in with some other works in the present Church [Worcester Cathedral], it is a diamond compared to a common pebble beauty contrasted with deformity. Green ascribes this to Sir John Beauchamp, from some painted arms formerly gory, emblems, and many other extraneous and in a window behind the monument. This was removed from the north aisle of the choir, where a skeleton was found in 1752, and also the date of 1292.
