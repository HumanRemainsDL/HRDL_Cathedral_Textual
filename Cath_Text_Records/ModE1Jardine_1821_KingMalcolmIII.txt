Malcolm was slain near the castle of Alnwick, in Northumberland, when besieging it, on the 13th of November 1093, in the 36th year of his reign. His body was deposited at Tinmouth [Tynemouth] by Earl Moubray, who unexpectedly attacked him when engaged at the siege of Alnwick. His remains were afterwards carried to Dunfermline.
"Rex Malcolmus (says Fortune,) postquam cæsus
fuerat, apud Tynemuth multis annis humatus
est -- et postmodum ab Alexandro filio suo Scotiam ad Dumfermelyn deportatus." 
['King Malcolm, (says Fortune), after he was felled, was buried near Tynemouth for many years, and afterwards was borne away to Dunfermline in Scotland by his son Alexander.']
