In the west vault, at the south end of the avenue, "William Higginbotham, Esq. died May 10th 1777
Aged 68 years." In this vault are the decayed remnants of two coffins, and the bones which they once contained are now rapidly returning to dust. In the centre vault, on the west side of the avenue.
On our last visit to the vaults, we found that the bones and fragments of the coffins had
