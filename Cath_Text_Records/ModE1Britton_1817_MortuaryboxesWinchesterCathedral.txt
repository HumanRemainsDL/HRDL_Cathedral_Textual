On the top of these screens [Winchester Cathedral] are six wooden chests, containing some memorials and relics of Saxon monarchs, princes, and other illustrious personages, former patrons of the Cathedral. The names are, Kynegils, Ethelwulf Escnin, Kentwin, Elmstan Kenulf Egbert, Adulfus, Canute, and Emma his queen, Alwyn, Wina, Stigand, Rufus, Edmund, eldest son of Alfred, Edred, &c. It may be remarked, that although these names appear on the chests, and we have pretty good authority that the persons they allude to were buried in the Cathedral; yet from the various changes and revolutions that have occurred in this Church, we can scarcely suppose that any remains of them can be identified.
